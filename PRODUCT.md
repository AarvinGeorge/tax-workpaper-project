# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CPA firm staff and their client, working one complex household's return (the invented Okafor-Lindqvist 2025 joint return):
- **Preparer**: works the file daily for hours; checks numbers against documents, fixes them, asks the client questions.
- **Reviewer and partner**: experienced CPAs (the 20-year CPA of the brief) who must trust the return fast: what changed, what is risky, what is still open, then sign off.
- **Client**: answers questions, uploads documents, approves and e-signs; not a tax professional.
- **Firm administrator**: standards, who viewed sensitive data, time against budget, status.
- **Next year's preparer** and an **IRS examiner years later**: read-only users who need the record to stand on its own.

Context: office laptops and monitors, long reading sessions of documents and numbers, interrupted work, deadlines.

## Product Purpose

A web-native tax workpaper that proves the return is right: for every number, where it came from, what was done to it,
who checked it, and what is still open. It replaces spreadsheets and PDF tickmarks with claims that carry their evidence,
checks that don't trust the engine, and a review flow with stages, send-backs, stale sign-offs and an audit trail.
Success is the brief's bar: **a 20-year CPA finds it faster and more trustworthy than anything they've used, and a new
hire can use it on day one.**

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. Quiet surfaces with the numbers and documents in front; nothing shouts unless it matters
(a failing check, a blocker, a stale sign-off). The voice of a careful senior reviewer: plain words, exact terms,
explains why a flag matters in one sentence.

Borrowed feel (patterns only, never code or assets): Claude/ChatGPT side panels (left history, centre work, right
document panel that follows the selection), GitHub pull requests (checks that must pass, resolved threads, approvals that
go stale), Linear (inbox triage, keyboard speed, peek panel), Figma/Notion (comments pinned to the exact spot, version
history).

## Anti-references

- Spreadsheets or grids of cells; traditional workpapers recreated in any form (the brief bans this).
- Legacy tax software: grey forms, tabs within tabs, modal dialogs everywhere.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: big-number KPI cards, gradients, identical card grids.
- Chat-first AI apps where everything goes through a chatbot instead of direct manipulation.

## Design Principles

1. **Every number answers four questions**: where from, what was done, who checked, what is open. One click away, always.
2. **Show the source, not a claim about the source**: the document with the field highlighted beats any label.
3. **Nothing important is hidden**: blockers, stale sign-offs and unverified items are visible where the work happens.
4. **Calm by default, loud for meaning**: colour and motion only mark status and risk.
5. **Learnable on day one, fast on day one thousand**: plain-language help on every flag, keyboard shortcuts shown in the command menu.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Light theme by default with a dark toggle. Full keyboard operation with visible focus. Status never shown by
colour alone (icon and word too). Tabular numerals for all amounts. Respect reduced-motion.
