## 5. UX: five worst problems

1. **Compare "v3 vs Finalized" shows the adjusted value in the v3 column** ("10,500, was 14,000" under V3, 10,500 under Finalized). A CPA reads the v3 column as the engine's number. Also "1 line differ".
2. **Actors appear as role keys and wall-clock times**: "daniel, 17 Oct 2026, 04:40 UTC" on Review and Changes while every story date is June 2026. Use the name and lead with story time.
3. **Home queue is stage-blind after filing**: at Filed/locked (Sam, next year's preparer) Home still says "Your queue: blockers first ... Blocking approval · 11" for a return already accepted. Next year's preparer should land on carryovers and the plan.
4. **Identity finding dumps raw JSON** as evidence: `Theo Lindqvist-Okafor · {"id":"theo","role":"dependent",...}`. Masked, but a new hire will not read JSON.
5. **Clutter**: Home lists the same ten blockers twice (Blocking approval, then Open checks); Marcus's Reveal tooltip quotes the firm rule twice; raw keys surface as labels ("Passive 1450 fremont", "Irs transcript pulled", "D71msg-10" run together on The fix); ledger ids (GT-A03) printed on the staff Checks inbox read as grader hints rather than product.
