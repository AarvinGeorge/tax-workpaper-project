To try it in two minutes:
1. Open the link. No sign-in: use **Viewing as** (top right) to switch between the preparer, reviewer, partner,
   clients and others.
2. As the reviewer, open **Checks** and click any amount: the right panel shows the source page with the field
   highlighted, the rule that built it, who checked it and what is open.
3. To start clean, add your own workspace name to the link, for example
   https://taxmark-workpaper.vercel.app/w/your-name/daniel (any name), or click **Reset to start**.

In one line: every number on the return carries its receipt, the app checks each one against a second source
instead of trusting the extraction, and AI reads the written text for problems while a person makes every call.
